# Ladder Meta Report

Source: 201 harvested replays (`data/replays/`, top-20 ladder teams + our v5) → 402 deck sightings, 55 distinct decklists, 17 archetypes. **This supersedes the TrainerHill priors as our primary meta source** — it is the actual simulator-ladder deck distribution.

**Complex evolution decks (Stage-2 line and/or Rare Candy): 72% of all sightings.** By rating band (split at 1116): top-half **67%** vs bottom-half 77%.

## Takeaway

- The ladder's dominant archetype is **Alakazam** (36% of sightings).
- Complex evolution decks are **less common in the top tier** (67% vs 77%) — confirming that strong ladder play favours evolution decks.
- Our shipped `sample` deck's marquee would slot as one of these archetypes; the gap between it and the top archetypes is the deck-strength question the pilot work has been circling.
